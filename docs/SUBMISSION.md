# Submission narrative draft

## Project description

THE ORIGIN is a browser-first research workspace that makes NASA microgravity combustion research easier to find, compare and question. It links every curated record to an official source and keeps published evidence separate from project interpretation. The prototype combines a research explorer, comparison table, local evidence retrieval, transparent research priorities and mission-oriented research questions.

## Problem

NASA combustion results span investigation catalogs, overview articles and technical documents. Reading across these sources requires attention to fuel, gravity environment, experimental context and uncertainty. A polished interface can become misleading if it hides missing measurements or presents hypotheses as established findings.

## Solution and distinction

Our contribution is a traceable workflow: every summary opens to a source reference, every missing numeric field stays missing, and every future application is labeled interpretation. Coverage maps expose the catalog's gaps. The tool helps organize investigation, not approve spacecraft safety. We do not claim that these interaction patterns or NASA findings are scientifically novel.

## What the demonstration proves

The initial application runs without installation or API credentials. It organizes nine curated records from five programs and seven source documents, plus 13 published test rows from two primary documents, filters them, compares their context, retrieves relevant source cards and explains an editorial priority score. It does not demonstrate a generative AI model, raw-data analysis, predictive simulation or validated safety recommendation.

## Technology and NASA data

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; Node's built-in tooling for cloud-side bundling and tests; no runtime package dependencies. Official NASA sources include NTRS Saffire VI metadata/abstract, BASS/BASS-II investigation PDF, FLEX article, ACME mission overview and PSI SAME-R catalog metadata. See METHODOLOGY.md for exact URLs, review scope and limitations.

## Concise presentation outline (draft, not final slides)

1. Problem: valuable combustion knowledge is scattered and context-sensitive.
2. Why it matters: future mission research needs traceable evidence and explicit uncertainties.
3. Product: one workspace for search, comparison, evidence and research priorities.
4. Method: curated official sources → structured records → local retrieval → cited views.
5. Demonstration: working filters, comparison and abstention; no invented measurements.
6. Limits and next steps: more primary data, numeric compatibility review, optional grounded generation and scientific review.
